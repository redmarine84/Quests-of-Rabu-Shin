Remove your OpenAI API key immediately

Open RabuShin → Settings → Remove Saved Key. This removes the encrypted OpenAI credential stored for your RabuShin Discord player account. You can also revoke the original key directly from your OpenAI account.

Request deletion of RabuShin player data

To request deletion of other stored RabuShin data, contact RabuShin Support and state that you are requesting a privacy/data deletion. For account verification, support may ask you to contact us while signed into the same Discord account or provide your Discord user ID.

Depending on campaign ownership and multiplayer relationships, deletion may require handling campaign ownership or shared campaign records before the player record can be removed. We will avoid exposing another player's private data while processing a request.

What a deletion request can cover

  • Discord player profile mapping used by RabuShin
  • characters and personal character state
  • encrypted OpenAI API-key data
  • journal and gameplay data associated with the account, subject to shared-campaign integrity and legal/security retention needs

Third-party data

Deleting RabuShin data does not automatically delete information held independently by Discord, OpenAI, Supabase, or other third-party services under their own accounts and policies.
