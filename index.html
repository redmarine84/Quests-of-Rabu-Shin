Quests of Rabu Shin: Tales of the Krasis

Created by Tim McIntyre

The Quests of Rabu Shin: Tales of the Krasis is an epic D&D 5e campaign spanning the twelve settlements of Vael Turog.

What begins with dying fields, mutated livestock, and unnatural whispers in Greymoor Hollow grows into a continent-wide struggle against the Krasis—engineered hybrids created through forced magical evolution.

The heroes investigate haunted seas, breathing crystals, flesh-growing forests, military sieges, sunken laboratories, political conspiracies, mobile mutation trials, frozen experiments, and ancient desert engines.

Each discovery reveals another piece of Rabu Shin's network and his belief that natural life is weak, unfinished, and meant to be redesigned.

The journey ultimately leads to Aetherfall, a floating arcane city transformed into a living evolutionary engine. There, the party must confront Rabu Shin, the Prime Krasis, and decide whether his power should be destroyed, controlled, preserved, or claimed—shaping the future of Vael Turog and determining whether evolution becomes salvation, tyranny, or catastrophe.

RabuShin AI Game Master

RabuShin AI Game Master is a Discord Activity for cooperative fantasy role-playing campaigns featuring an AI-powered Game Master.

Players can participate in multiplayer campaigns, create and manage characters, communicate with other campaign members, manage inventories and spellbooks, and interact with the AI Game Master as their adventure progresses.
