This Privacy Policy explains how ("RabuShin," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the RabuShin AI Game Master Discord Activity.

Information we process

  • Discord account information: Discord user ID, username, and display name used to identify your RabuShin player account and campaign membership.
  • Game data: campaigns, campaign codes, characters, ability scores, inventory, equipment state, spellbooks, journals, campaign chat, AI Game Master messages, and related gameplay state.
  • OpenAI API key: if you choose to use AI Game Master features, you provide your own OpenAI API key. The RabuShin server tests the key, encrypts it before storage, and stores only the encrypted value in the RabuShin database. The saved key is not returned to the browser after storage. The server decrypts it only when needed to authenticate your requests to OpenAI.
  • Technical information: server logs, request errors, timestamps, and security/diagnostic information that may be processed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate players, operate multiplayer campaigns, save game progress, provide AI Game Master functionality, enforce server-authoritative game rules, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the service.

Third-party services

RabuShin relies on third-party services to operate. Depending on your use, information may be processed by:

  • Discord for Activity hosting context and Discord identity authentication.
  • Supabase for RabuShin database storage.
  • OpenAI for AI Game Master requests made with the API key you provide.
  • Hosting/network providers used to deliver the public web application and backend services.

Your use of those services may also be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

OpenAI API-key security

Your OpenAI API key is treated as a credential. It is sent to the RabuShin backend over the application's secure connection, validated, encrypted server-side, and stored as encrypted data. Other campaign players cannot retrieve it through RabuShin. You may remove the saved key at any time in RabuShin Settings.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Do not reuse an API key that you no longer trust, and revoke compromised keys through your OpenAI account.

Retention

Game data is retained while needed to provide RabuShin and until it is deleted, the related campaign is deleted, or a valid deletion request is completed. A saved OpenAI API key remains stored in encrypted form until you remove it or your player data is deleted. Operational logs may be retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

Sharing

We do not sell RabuShin user data. Data is shared only as needed to provide the service, comply with law, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, or when you intentionally share content with other members of your multiplayer campaign.

Your choices

  • Remove your saved OpenAI API key in the RabuShin Settings tab.
  • Choose what you write in campaign chat, journals, and AI Game Master messages.
  • Request deletion of RabuShin data using the Data Deletion page.

Children

RabuShin is intended only for users who are old enough to use Discord under Discord's applicable requirements. We do not knowingly design the service for children below Discord's minimum permitted age.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as RabuShin changes. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.
